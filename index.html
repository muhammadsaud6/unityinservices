About Dr. Patricia Rogers

Through speaking platforms, Podcast interviews and collaboration books for over a decade, Unity In Service, Inc. provides powerful platforms for entrepreneurs to be seen, heard, and celebrated. Having hosted hundreds of virtual and in-person speaking platforms, providing thousands of speaking opportunities for thriving entrepreneurs.

Dr. Patricia Rogers, CEO, & Visibility Strategist, is a 2x International Award-Winning Public Speaker, and 3x International Best-Selling Author. She is the CEO of Unity In Service, which launched in 2012, setting a platform to bring entrepreneurs together so they can create wealth and provide solutions through the use of resources and collaboration.

With 29 years in law enforcement and 12 years in the US Army, she now trains and empowers entrepreneurs. Business Coach, Social Media Marketer and Promoter, Event Host & Organizer, Dr. Rogers is unwaveringly dedicated to assisting entrepreneurs in building profitable relationships and excelling in their businesses.
